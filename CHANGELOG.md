# Changelog

Product versions of Vekipper and when they shipped. This is a summary for readers outside the project. The detailed changelog lives in the private source repository.

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org). Vekipper is pre-1.0: minor versions add features, and patch versions fix or polish them.

## 0.23.1 · 2026-11-18
- The public homepage is now fully static and served from the CDN, in Spanish and English. The waitlist counter updates when someone joins or leaves.

## 0.23.0 · 2026-11-15
- **The website in English** at vekipper.com/en: homepage, privacy, terms, help, contact, feedback and about. The language is picked from your country and browser, with a switch to change it.
- Waitlist welcome emails in the language you signed up in.
- Clean addresses for each homepage section (/en/pricing, /en/features…).

## 0.22.0 · 2026-11-14
- **Waitlist until the relaunch:** the app closes while Google and Microsoft finish their reviews. People who join get founder pricing (first three months of Pro at half price).
- A new website with the Vekipper brand: colors, type, logo and an AI demo.
- Feedback without an account, and a comparison with similar apps.

## 0.21.1 · 2026-11-10
- Canonical addresses and structured data for search engines.

## 0.21.0 · 2026-11-10
- **Calendar keyboard shortcuts**, and a faster calendar when switching views or weeks.
- Redesigned *New event* and *New task* dialogs, with custom pickers for dates, times, calendars and repeats.
- Smoother dragging and transitions. Better screen-reader labels for events.

## 0.20.0 · 2026-11-10
- **Appearance settings saved to your account:** hour height, 12/24-hour clock, weekends and dimmed past events.
- A resizable task column, and booking a time slot for a task without dragging.
- A lighter, more accessible task board, with keyboard reordering and undo on complete.

## 0.19.0 · 2026-11-10
- **Dark mode:** light, dark or automatic.
- The AI daily limit resets at your local midnight.
- Complete Pro terms and a revised privacy policy.

## 0.18.0 · 2026-11-09
- **Pro subscriptions with Polar** (USD 6/month or USD 60/year). The AI becomes part of Pro, with a daily limit.

## 0.17.3 · 2026-11-09
- Domain association for Microsoft publisher verification.

## 0.17.2 · 2026-11-09
- Google sign-in now completes on vekipper.com, as Google's verification requires.

## 0.17.1 · 2026-11-08
- Incremental authorization for Google permissions.

## 0.17.0 · 2026-11-06
- **AI command center (Ctrl/⌘ + K)** with AI Calendar, AI Planner and AI Summary.
- AI Calendar handles several items in one sentence and asks when a time is ambiguous.
- AI Planner explains each block, respects your working hours and lunch, and learns which parts of the day you reject.
- AI Summary leads with what matters most and flags conflicts and yesterday's leftovers.

## 0.16.0 · 2026-11-06
- **A collapsible sidebar** replaces the *Today* column.
- **Tasks on the calendar:** a task dropped on the week becomes its time block, and completing or moving the block updates the task.
- A daily summary on the first visit of the day, and holiday warnings.

## 0.15.0 · 2026-11-02
- A new landing page with real screenshots and the exact Google and Microsoft permissions Vekipper asks for.
- Help, contact, about and data-rights pages.

## 0.14.0 · 2026-11-01
- First AI features, in private testing: a daily summary and a day planner.
- Free and Pro plans announced. Tags removed: lists organize tasks.

## 0.13.0 · 2026-11-01
- **Vekipper becomes a day planner without email.** Gmail and Outlook mail are removed, and Google is asked only for Calendar and Tasks.
- A *Today* column with what's on now, the day's agenda and tasks due today.

## 0.12.0 · 2026-10-31
- A full redesign: color only where it matters, translucent surfaces and a lighter week view.

## 0.11.0 · 2026-10-31
- Outlook mail next to Gmail. *(Removed in 0.13.0.)*

## 0.10.0 · 2026-10-31
- **Microsoft To Do** on the same board as Google Tasks, with steps, importance, reminders and repeats.
- Move tasks between Google Tasks and To Do.

## 0.9.0 · 2026-10-31
- **Plan on the week:** jump to any date, create and move events by dragging, and drag tasks onto the week to block time.

## 0.8.0 · 2026-10-30
- **Microsoft arrives:** sign in with Microsoft, and Outlook Calendar in the same week as Google Calendar, with read, write and live updates.

## 0.7.2 · 2026-10-30
- Own domain, vekipper.com. Security headers and automated dependency updates.

## 0.7.1 · 2026-10-28

## 0.7.0 · 2026-10-28

## 0.6.0 · 2026-10-28
- **Live updates:** calendar changes arrive in seconds through provider notifications, and the dashboard keeps itself up to date.

## 0.5.0 · 2026-10-28

## 0.4.0 · 2026-10-27
